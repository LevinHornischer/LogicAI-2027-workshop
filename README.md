# Idea and Motivation

Logic and AI have a long history, sometimes in close contact (during the classical phase of AI), sometimes drifting apart (since the deep learning revolution). Recent issues in AI—e.g., interpretability, trustworthiness, or theorem proving—call for a closer interaction of logic and AI. And much is indeed happening! The idea of the workshop is to bring together researchers who are interested in logic and AI to discuss the latest developments. 

The workshop will feature two talks by experts in the field, as well as a presentation of the forthcoming edited book "Logic and AI Today" which surveys the current logic and AI interface. The workshop also has a dedicated slot at the end for discussion among the participants.
